Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Do you accept reservations?

Yes! We highly recommend making a reservation, especially for weekends and special occasions. You can book a table by calling us at +254 711 739 233 or by filling out the contact form above. For large groups (8+ people), please book at least 24 hours in advance.

Do you offer catering services?

Absolutely! We provide catering services for events of all sizes, from intimate gatherings to large corporate events. Contact us to discuss your specific needs and we'll create a custom menu that will make your event memorable.

Is there parking available?

Yes, we have ample parking space available for our guests. Garden City also offers additional parking facilities. Valet parking is available on weekends and during special events.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Certainly! We're happy to accommodate various dietary needs including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and other allergies. Please inform us of any dietary restrictions when making your reservation so we can prepare accordingly.

Do you host private events?

Yes! Our restaurant can be reserved for private events including birthday parties, corporate meetings, wedding receptions, and more. We offer customized packages to suit your needs. Contact us for more information and pricing.
